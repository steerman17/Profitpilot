How this works

Project cost = materials + (hours × hourly labor cost) + other costs. Suggested price = project cost ÷ (1 − target margin). Gross profit = price − cost. A 30% margin is different from a 30% markup.

For planning only. Include applicable taxes, insurance, transaction fees, travel, and other costs in your inputs. This page does not collect or transmit your entries; calculations happen in your browser. It has no accounts or payments.
